# Castle Print artwork and sources

The thirteen SVG files in this directory are original geometry authored for
Knight Chess: a castle-room knight illustration plus six chess silhouettes in
two colorways. No third-party illustration, stock texture, game branding, traced
composition or generated bitmap is used. The ink strokes, arch cuts, window,
carved chessman, candle, score slip and board inlays are deliberately placed.

Art references were conceptual: the confidence of a distinctive game title,
strong silhouette, clear hierarchy and an inhabited atmosphere. No Balatro or
Hades assets or branding are included. This is an original quiet castle-print
interpretation, not a reproduction of either game's art style or composition.

Existing DejaVu font files are unchanged; their redistribution terms remain in
assets/FONT-LICENSE.txt. Existing ability SVG and rarity/slot semantics are
unchanged. There are no new third-party asset dependencies.

Palette: parchment #ead7b2, ink #20363e, copper #b66a4d, board ivory #dfc89e,
slate #698284. White and Black pieces retain contrasting filled silhouettes.
Green marks legal movement, blue inspection, amber threats, gold last move,
red check; teal/violet/gold ability tier meanings remain unchanged.
